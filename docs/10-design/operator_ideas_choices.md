# Operator Ideas: Making Choices Matter

> Philosophy: Give users operators that make their choices matter. Since puzzles have single solutions, operators must reward HOW you play (skill, strategy, risk) not WHAT the puzzle contains.

## Player Choices That Matter

1. **Order of deletion** - which cells to click first
2. **Speed vs accuracy** - rush for time bonus or play safe
3. **Circle usage** - mark cells or rely on memory
4. **Restore usage** - how many undos/mistakes
5. **Row vs column focus** - which direction to prioritize
6. **Shop decisions** - which operators, when to skip
7. **Money management** - save for later or spend now
8. **Risk tolerance** - safe play vs gambling for bonuses
9. **Operator synergies** - building combos

## 25 New Operator Ideas

### Category A: Order & Sequence

#### 1. Row First
**Effect:** +50 points for each row completed before any column is completed
**Rarity:** Uncommon | **Price:** 6

**Why it's fun:** Forces a specific playstyle. Players must consciously sequence their deletions to complete rows while keeping columns incomplete. Creates tension as you get close to accidentally completing a column.

**Choice impact:** Changes your entire approach to the puzzle. Do you buy this and commit to row-first play, or skip it if you prefer flexible play?

---

#### 2. Column First
**Effect:** +50 points for each column completed before any row is completed
**Rarity:** Uncommon | **Price:** 6

**Why it's fun:** Mirror of Row First. Some puzzles are easier row-first, others column-first. Having both in the shop creates meaningful "read the puzzle" decisions.

**Choice impact:** Evaluate the puzzle layout before committing. Creates operator vs operator tension in shop.

---

#### 3. Streak Master
**Effect:** +10 points per consecutive correct deletion (resets on restore)
**Rarity:** Rare | **Price:** 8

**Why it's fun:** Rewards flow state. 10 correct clicks = +55 cumulative bonus. Creates mounting tension—do you keep the streak or play safe?

**Choice impact:** Every click matters more. Encourages entering a confident, focused state rather than tentative clicking.

---

#### 4. Hot Streak
**Effect:** ×1.5 score if you complete puzzle with 8+ consecutive deletions
**Rarity:** Rare | **Price:** 9

**Why it's fun:** All-or-nothing multiplier. Encourages planning your deletion order to end on a streak.

**Choice impact:** Shapes how you approach the endgame of each puzzle. Save "easy" cells for the finish.

---

#### 5. Alternator
**Effect:** +30 points each time you alternate row/column completion (row→col→row...)
**Rarity:** Uncommon | **Price:** 6

**Why it's fun:** Requires weaving between rows and columns. Some puzzles make this easy, others hard. Skill expression through ordering.

**Choice impact:** Do you take this and commit to alternating, or buy something more flexible?

---

### Category B: Speed & Timing

#### 6. Blitz
**Effect:** +200 points if puzzle solved in under 15 seconds
**Rarity:** Rare | **Price:** 7

**Why it's fun:** Creates high-tension speedrun moments. Not every puzzle is blitzable—sizing up the puzzle becomes a skill.

**Choice impact:** Do you rush and risk mistakes, or play safe and forfeit the bonus? Decision every puzzle.

---

#### 7. Slow and Steady
**Effect:** +100 points if puzzle takes over 45 seconds (no time pressure)
**Rarity:** Common | **Price:** 4

**Why it's fun:** Anti-speed-run. Rewards methodical play. Synergy with circle-heavy strategies.

**Choice impact:** Explicitly conflicts with Blitz. Player must choose a playstyle. Good for anxious players.

---

#### 8. Early Bird
**Effect:** +25 points for each cell deleted in the first 10 seconds
**Rarity:** Uncommon | **Price:** 5

**Why it's fun:** Encourages quick initial reads. Players develop "snap judgment" skills about obvious deletions.

**Choice impact:** Do you trust your first instincts? Rewards players who can quickly identify non-critical cells.

---

#### 9. Countdown
**Effect:** +5 points per second remaining when puzzle completes
**Rarity:** Common | **Price:** 4

**Why it's fun:** Simple speed incentive. 30 seconds left = +150. Stacks with other speed operators.

**Choice impact:** Constant micro-decision: "Am I fast enough to earn this bonus or should I slow down?"

---

#### 10. Momentum
**Effect:** Each deletion within 1 second of previous: +15 points
**Rarity:** Uncommon | **Price:** 6

**Why it's fun:** Rewards rapid-fire confident play. Creates a "rhythm game" feel within the puzzle.

**Choice impact:** Encourages developing pattern recognition and muscle memory. Practice pays off.

## Synergy Map

These operators create interesting decision trees:

```
Speed Path:          Precision Path:       Gambling Path:
├─ Blitz             ├─ Surgeon            ├─ Double or Nothing
├─ Early Bird        ├─ Clean Sweep        ├─ Glass Cannon
├─ Countdown         ├─ Safety Net         ├─ Perfectionist's Gambit
└─ Momentum          └─ Streak Insurance   └─ Hedge Fund

Circle Path:         Sequence Path:        Economy Path:
├─ Circle Scholar    ├─ Row First          ├─ Compound Interest
├─ Circlicious       ├─ Column First       ├─ Loan Shark
└─ vs Minimalist     ├─ Alternator         ├─ Life Insurance
   vs Purist         └─ Streak Master      └─ Overflow Slot
```
